1. Problem Framing & Operational Decision ML-03
Decision Supported

Prioritizing Diagnostic Editorial Attention

In enterprise digital publishing, organizations manage content portfolios spanning thousands of URLs. Organic search traffic fluctuates over time, but editorial teams operate under strictly finite diagnostic bandwidth and cannot manually investigate every page with equal depth.

This system supports candidate queue prioritization for human editorial content review. The model outputs a predicted likelihood of organic search traffic decay to rank pages, enabling editors to allocate structured diagnostic review where empirical vulnerability signals are concentrated.

Model Signal → Review Priority → Human Diagnosis → Editorial Action
Operating Asymmetry

Unit of Analysis & Evaluation Grain

  • Unit of Analysis: Published URL (content_hash_id) within client (client_hash_id) evaluated over May 1–31, 2026.
  • Target Outcome: Binary indicator if May clicks drop by ≥ 20% relative to April.
  • Evaluation Metric: Precision@50 (proportion of true declining pages in the top 50 ranked candidates).
  • False Positive Cost: Allocates ~2–5h of planning diagnostic effort to a page that stays above the decay threshold.
  • False Negative Cost: Lower review priority for a declining page.
2. Data Safety & Transparent Baseline ML-04 · ML-07
Data Contract & Leakage Boundaries

79M+ Rows of Enterprise Search Data

The dataset covers 79M+ rows of FlyRank enterprise search-performance data across the warehouse client population (fact_content_daily_performance table, build v20260703). To focus on pages with measurable search presence, we applied two activity-based criteria:

  • GSC Impressions (Feb–Apr) ≥ 1,000
  • GSC Clicks (Apr) ≥ 10

Yields N = 16,513 eligible pages across 36 client domains (Portfolio base rate = 41.62% decline). Historical features are strictly bounded to Feb 1 – Apr 30, 2026, quarantined from May 1–31 forward outcomes.

The published analysis and repository artifacts contain no client domain names, raw URLs, private search queries, or personally identifiable information.

Non-Learned Benchmark

Week 4 Heuristic Baseline Rule

To establish a transparent, non-learned benchmark, the baseline averages daily April signals: GSC impressions ≥ 10, average position > 10, and GA4 pageviews ≥ 1.

Daily Score = 1{gsc_impressions ≥ 10} + 1{gsc_avg_position > 10} + 1{ga4_pageviews ≥ 1} Baseline Score = mean(Daily Score across April records)

Deterministic Tie-Breaking Cascade:
Score DESC → April Impr DESC → April Pageviews DESC → Avg Position ASC → content_hash_id ASC

Primary Split Result: Precision@50 = 0.2000 (10/50 true declining pages).

Controlled Leakage Audit

Future Information Injection Check

To verify validation integrity, we ran a controlled leakage test injecting post-April (May 2026) search clicks into feature matrices:

  • Clean W05 Model: Precision@50 = 0.2400 (12/50) | ROC-AUC: 0.4594
  • Leaky Model (May Injected): Precision@50 = 1.0000 (50/50) | ROC-AUC: 0.8921

The artificial performance jump demonstrates why strict temporal boundaries between historical feature windows and outcome evaluation windows are essential.

Top-Queue Error Analysis

Inspection of False Positives (Seed 42)

At Precision@50 = 0.2400 on the held-out test set:

  • True Positives (12 / 50, 24%): Correctly flagged pages experiencing ≥ 20% click decay.
  • False Positives (38 / 50, 76%): Top-ranked pages that did not meet the study's May decline label.

The false-positive analysis shows that several highly ranked candidates did not meet the study's May decline label, reinforcing that the model's ranking signal should be treated as a review-prioritization aid rather than a diagnosis.

5. Human-in-the-Loop Safety & Methodological Limits ML-10 · ML-11
Safety Guardrails

The Anti-Automation No-Go List

The system enforces universal safety guardrails: MODEL_SCORE_DOES_NOT_AUTHORIZE_REDIRECT_OR_CANONICAL_CHANGE.

  • No Autonomous LLM Rewriting: Model outputs must never trigger unsupervised automated content regeneration or publishing.
  • No Programmatic Redirects: High decay probability cannot trigger automated 301 redirects or canonical tags.
  • No Automated Page Deletion: Low traffic signals must never trigger automated URL pruning or unpublishing.
  • Mandatory Senior Signoff: Pages with P ≥ 0.70 or in sensitive YMYL domains require senior editorial oversight.
Limitations

What This Work Cannot Claim

  • Association ≠ Causation: The model identifies statistical correlations in historical search data; it cannot establish that a specific content edit will causally reverse decay.
  • Partition Sensitivity: Lift varied between -12.0 pp and +14.0 pp across client partitions. The single-split +4.0 pp lift is not a universal guarantee.
  • High False Positive Rate: At 24% Precision@50, 38 of 50 candidates do not decline ≥ 20%. The tool is strictly for candidate triage, not automated diagnosis.
  • No Search Algorithm Reverse-Engineering: The system models historical performance in this dataset, not Google's proprietary ranking algorithm.
